Can the web app read what you were actually playing?

One question only: given a time window — a run — can this page recover the tracks that were genuinely playing on your phone during it, without you telling it anything? Nothing here touches cadence, coaching, or the calibration test.

One-time setup

Spotify needs you to register a free app to get a Client ID. It takes about two minutes, and it has to be you — I can't accept their developer terms on your behalf.

  1. Open the Spotify Developer Dashboard and log in.
  2. Click Create app. Name and description can be anything.
  3. For Redirect URI, paste exactly this and click Add:
    —
  4. Under "Which API/SDKs are you planning to use", tick Web API.
  5. Save, then open Settings and copy the Client ID (not the secret — you never need the secret here, and you should never paste it into a web page).
